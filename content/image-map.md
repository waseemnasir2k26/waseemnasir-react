# Image map

The old image map referred to files that were retired in the 2026-11-17 redesign.
Live photos are in `public/img/v2/` (WebP/JPG, EXIF stripped, max 1800px long edge).
They are referenced by short filename through `IMG()` in `lib/design.ts`, plus
`app/blog/posts.ts` (post heroes and OG crops) and `app/layout.tsx` (Person JSON-LD image).
Source library: `WASEEM IMAGES/PROFESSIONAL` (never use `_REMOVED*`, `_DUPLICATES`, `_REJECT`;
owner-banned shots are tracked in memory, not in this repo).
